# Trademark policy

The 3BCAD code is open source under the Apache-2.0 license. The names **3BCAD** and
**Three Bugs Studio**, and their logos (`assets/`), are not: they say where a program comes from, and
the code licenses do not cover them (Apache-2.0, section 6).

## You may, without asking

- Use the names to talk about 3BCAD truthfully: in articles, videos, courses and reviews, or to say your
  software "works with 3BCAD files".
- Share unmodified official releases from
  [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Three-Bugs-Studio/3bcad/releases) under their own name.
- Say that your project is **based on 3BCAD by Three Bugs Studio**, with a link to
  https://github.com/Three-Bugs-Studio/3bcad. We ask every fork and derived product to say so.

## Please do not

- Call a modified version, a fork or a product built from it "3BCAD", or use a name or logo that can be
  mistaken for it. Give it its own name and logo.
- Suggest that Three Bugs Studio made, checked or endorses your version when it did not.
- Remove the copyright notices, the license files or the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file from copies of the code.
  The licenses require them to stay.

## Questions

Ask in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Three-Bugs-Studio/3bcad/issues) or through the contact on the
organization profile. Tell us about copies that remove the notices or use the name on a modified
version; we will ask them to fix it first.
